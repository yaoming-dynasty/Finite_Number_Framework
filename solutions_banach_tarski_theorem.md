## 2. The Core Proof Mechanism: Free Groups and Rotations

*   **The Mechanism:** The proof shows that $SO(3)$ contains a free group generated by two rotations. Because a free group allows infinite, non-commuting sequences of operations without collapse, points on the sphere can be shifted into overlapping, replicated configurations that defy standard measure.
*   **The Vulnerability:** Standard group theory treats group operations as purely formal string manipulations without systemic feedback on "mass" or "relational scale," allowing abstract infinite divergence to manifest as physical volume duplication.

## 4. Framework Resolution 2: Enforcing the Balance Gate ($\text{Bal}$)

*   **The Framework Principle:** **Axiom 1** and the Balance Gate ($\text{Bal}$) require that the net sum of a closed inversion set resolves to zero ($\sum \{x \to B\} = 0$).
*   **The Resolution:** The free group construction in Banach-Tarski relies on partitioning sets into unequal fractional ratios (e.g., pieces that recombine into 2 wholes from 1). Under the framework, any asymmetric multiplication of components without a corresponding inverse deficit ($\text{-1}$ / Few) creates an un-neutralized surplus. The Balance Gate flags this divergence as a systemic imbalance, invalidating the free group's capacity to create matter or space out of nothing.
